Using JustTaken

Delete your account and data

Delete your account in the app, or start a request without using the app.

Delete in the app

Sign in to JustTaken. Open Settings, choose “Delete account and data,” and review the information before proceeding. Once your request is accepted, you are signed out, access from other devices and sharing links is disabled, and deletion of stored data proceeds in stages.

Deletion cannot be undone. Save any photos, videos, and capture proofs you need before starting.

Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can start a request using the contact below. Tell us that you want your JustTaken account and associated data deleted.

Sending an email starts a request. Deletion proceeds after identity and account verification. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, or identity document images.

Data covered by deletion

This includes account information; photos, videos, and previews stored in JustTaken; capture records, location information and capture proofs; posts and sharing settings; follow and block relationships; terms-agreement records; related reports and moderation records; device registrations; and sign-in information.

Data being uploaded is also covered. We recheck and repeat deletion after the first pass to prevent overlapping operations from restoring data. Failures are retried, so acceptance of a request and completion of stored-data deletion do not happen simultaneously.

Retained information and deletion limits

  • To prevent revival of a deleted account, we retain only a hash derived from your account ID and its expiry time for 31 days. You cannot register again using the same sign-in method during that period. Protection is extended until completion if deletion remains unfinished.
  • Information needed to receive and retry the request is retained during processing and removed on completion. Recovery backups and existing operational logs may retain information until their respective retention periods end.
  • JustTaken cannot retrieve or delete files and certificates saved by you or recipients on their devices, or proof information already registered in external public audit logs.

If you also want your early-registration email deleted, tell us and provide the registered email address. We handle that separately from account deletion.
