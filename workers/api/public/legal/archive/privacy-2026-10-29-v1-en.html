03Early-registration information

We collect the following from people requesting a release announcement:

The early-registration practices in this section are unchanged from version 2026-10-25.2. The early-registration agreement version is managed separately from the app agreement version.

  • Email address.
  • Intended device: iPhone, Android, or both.
  • The version of the agreed information, registration time, and retention deadline.
  • A hashed value of the secret code used to cancel registration.

We use this information to accept registrations, check duplicates, announce the release, cancel registrations, and prevent fraudulent registrations. We do not use early-registration email addresses for advertising or sell them. Registration details are not made public. We are not currently sending registration confirmation emails.

The retention period is 180 days from registration. You can delete a registration using the cancellation link in your saved receipt. Registering the same email again does not renew the original retention deadline or cancellation link.

04Website and verification-page information

For site delivery, prevention of unauthorized access, and troubleshooting, our infrastructure providers, such as Cloudflare, process IP addresses, access times, request destinations, and browser and connection information. Our landing page does not use advertising tracking tags or analytics tools.

Original photos and videos selected on the proof-verification page are checked within your browser. Their contents and file names are not uploaded to our server. Verification may retrieve our public keys and shared proofs or query an independent public audit log. Connection information, such as your IP address, is transmitted to those destinations.

A proof file contains the original’s hash, format, size, capture time and other metadata, a proof ID, and a pseudonymous ID associated with the capture account. This pseudonymous ID does not indicate verified real-world identity, but proofs bearing the same ID may be linked.

06Location, camera, and microphone settings

Location is off by default. We obtain location while using the app camera only if you enable it and grant device permission. We do not continuously track location in the background. Depending on OS settings, the location may be approximate. Capture can continue without location if it is unavailable.

When you choose to save an original and its proof after capture, the collected location is sent to the server with the capture record and stored as a record only you can view. Coordinates collected by JustTaken are not added to social posts, shared proofs, the original’s EXIF, independent timestamp requests, or public audit logs for publication.

Turning location off applies to future captures and does not alter previous signed records. To erase records containing past locations, delete the relevant originals and capture proofs. Camera, microphone, and location permissions can be revoked in device settings. Features you do not authorize may be unavailable.

07Audiences and sharing destinations

Accounts are private by default. Originals, posts, and proofs are handled according to your chosen save, publication, and sharing actions. Content you choose to make public may be viewable by an unspecified audience. Consider any people, documents, addresses, or other details in the content before changing its audience.

Proof links share proof information, not the original file. To give someone the original, export and share it separately. Recipients and destination services may save or forward files, and we cannot remotely delete their copies. Check the destination service’s policies as well.

We do not sell personal information. We do not provide personal data to third parties except for publication or sharing you choose, with your consent, where required by law, where necessary to protect life, physical safety, or property and obtaining consent is difficult, or in other circumstances permitted by law.

08External services and processing abroad

  • Cloudflare: Website and API delivery, databases, original-file storage, and security. It processes early-registration and app information as a service provider to the extent needed to provide features.
  • Apple and Google: Selected external sign-in methods and checks of device and app authenticity. The app communicates with these services when the relevant features are used after release.
  • Sigstore timestamping and the Rekor public log: Used to support capture-proof timestamps and records. See the next section for submitted information and deletion limitations.

External services include businesses outside Japan, and information may be processed in the United States and other countries or regions. For transfers of personal data abroad, we check recipient safeguards, supervise service providers, and provide information and obtain consent as required by law. Contact us for information about overseas processing and safeguards.

External privacy policies: Cloudflare, Apple, and Google.

09Independent timestamps and public audit logs

External records for capture proofs use hashes computed from proofs, random values, and our service’s signatures and public keys. We do not submit original photos, videos or audio, previews, file names, email addresses, location coordinates, capture-account IDs, or the proof certificate body itself for log registration.

Registered public audit-log information is public and generally cannot be withdrawn or deleted. Registered hashes and similar information remain after deletion of an original or account. Our service’s public key may allow multiple entries to be associated, and registration times and counts may be observable. This does not mean that an original’s contents are published in the log.

10Device and browser storage

  • The landing page saves the logo visibility setting in browser local storage. This display setting is not sent to our server.
  • The verification page saves fingerprints associated with proof IDs and public-log checkpoints in local storage to help compare replacement proofs or changes to the log.
  • The app stores sign-in state, language, logo and location settings, unsent capture data, and temporary export files on your device.

You can remove stored information through browser or device settings. Removing verification history means that device can no longer compare against previously seen proofs. Deleting device data is separate from deleting originals and proofs stored on the server.

11Retention and deletion

  • Early registration: Expires 180 days after registration and is removed by periodic processing. You can delete it sooner using the cancellation link. Contact us if you have lost that link.
  • Originals and capture proofs: Retained when you choose to save them. Deleting an original and proof deletes the original and related posts and disables proof links. Deleting only a social post leaves separately saved originals and proofs in place.
  • Location: Removed from current server records when the original and proof are deleted. Turning the setting off does not erase past records.
  • Account deletion: You can request deletion of your account and data in app settings. Once accepted, access from all devices and sharing links is disabled. Originals, posts, capture records, locations, proofs, agreements, follow and block relationships, and related reports and moderation records are deleted in stages. Rechecks and retries address overlapping operations and failures, so acceptance and completion are not simultaneous. You can also use the deletion request contact without signing in or using the app.
  • Preventing reuse after deletion: A hash derived from your account ID and its expiry time are retained for 31 days to prevent revival of a deleted account. During this period you cannot register again using the same sign-in method. Protection is extended until deletion completes if processing remains unfinished. Information needed to receive and retry the request is removed when processing completes. We cannot erase external public audit logs or copies saved by third parties.
  • Backups and temporary files: Recovery backups, existing operational logs, and device drafts and exported files have retention periods separate from active data. Deletion does not mean every copy disappears immediately. Delete files saved on your device or sharing destinations yourself as needed.

Other information is handled for the period and scope necessary to provide features, answer inquiries, maintain security, and comply with law; information no longer needed is subject to deletion or other appropriate measures. Early-registration email addresses are managed separately from app accounts. To delete an early registration as well, use its cancellation link or contact us.

12Security and inquiry information

We take measures against unauthorized access, disclosure, and alteration, including encrypted communications, authentication, access restrictions, and protection of confidential information. We review operator access, service-provider supervision, and procedures according to the information and work involved. In the event of a breach or similar incident, we investigate, report, and notify affected people as required by law.

For inquiries, we collect your name, contact details, inquiry, and information needed to respond. We use these to respond, verify identity, address rights infringements, and maintain records. We do not request excessive identity documents, passwords, or sign-in codes.

13Requests concerning personal data

Under applicable law, you may request notice of purposes of use; disclosure; correction, addition, or deletion; cessation of use or erasure; cessation of third-party provision; and disclosure of third-party provision records for personal data we hold. Contact us below with your request and information identifying the registered email address or relevant records.

We verify, as necessary, that you are the person concerned or an authorized representative and respond in accordance with law. If we cannot fulfill part of a request, we explain why. Identity verification procedures are explained individually when you contact us. If you have an early-registration cancellation link, you can cancel without contacting us.

14Policy changes

We update this policy as information handling or features change. Significant changes and their effective dates are announced through the website, app, or similar channels. If renewed consent is legally required for a change in purpose or third-party provision, we obtain it separately.

Updating this page does not automatically repurpose previous early registrations. The earlier early-registration policy of October 25, 2026 and previous version 2026-10-25.2 are available in Japanese. The app separately asks for agreement to the new version; past agreement records are not automatically rewritten.
